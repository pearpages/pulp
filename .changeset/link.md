Link (experimental): a text link with `tone` (`action`, `default`, `muted`), `underline` (`always`, `hover`) and `asChild`, so a router's own link component takes the styles and keeps client-side navigation. Underlined by default: colour alone does not tell a link from the text around it. Tokens: `--link-*`.
