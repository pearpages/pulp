NavList: a vertical list of links for an app's sidebar or a section's pages. A labelled `nav` with a list of links, the page marked `aria-current="page"` and its section `aria-current="true"`, a nested list for a section's pages (`sub`), an optional icon and badge per link, and `asChild` for a router's link or a `button`. No hooks, so it renders on the server. Tokens: `--nav-list-*`.
