Divider (experimental): a hairline between two groups, horizontal or vertical, with `spacing` on the spacing scale. Decorative by default (`role="none"`); `decorative={false}` makes it a `separator`. Tokens: `--divider-color`, `--divider-thickness`, `--divider-spacing-{sm,md,lg}`.
