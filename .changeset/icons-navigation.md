Eight glyphs bitepals drew by hand: `Globe` (language and region), `Newspaper` (a feed), `UserPlus` (a follow request, an invite), `UserCheck` (someone you follow), `Flag` (want to go), `Lightbulb` (a recommendation), `Eye` and `EyeOff` (shown and hidden). Same 24 grid and 2 px round stroke as the rest. For a two-tone look, set `fill` and `color` from your own CSS: the glyph's shapes take both.
