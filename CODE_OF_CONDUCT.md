# Code of conduct

pulp follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique the work and not the person, and make room for
people who are new. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull
requests, discussions, or anywhere else the project lives.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@pearpages](https://github.com/pearpages), privately
on GitHub. Reports are read by the maintainer alone and handled in confidence. The maintainer may
edit or remove comments, and block contributors, who break these rules.
